Frequently asked questions

How does paying to snooze work?

When an alarm rings, tap Snooze and pay with your Apple ID to sleep nine more minutes. Each snooze in a row costs more. If you don't pay within a minute, the alarm rings again. Stopping the alarm is always free, and you get one free snooze every week.

Can I get a refund for a snooze?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My alarm didn't ring.

Make sure alarm access is on in Settings → Nine More. Alarms ring even in Silent mode and Focus.

What happens if I sleep through an alarm?

Nothing is charged. It counts like a snooze for your alarm streak, so the streak resets unless you have a streak freeze.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings → Nine More → Language on your iPhone.
