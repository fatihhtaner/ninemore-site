Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Nine More ("the app") keeps your information on your own device. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Data on your device

Your alarms, labels, statistics, points, streak and settings are stored only on your iPhone. We, the developer, cannot access them.

No accounts, ads or tracking

Nine More does not require an account, does not show ads, does not use analytics, and does not track you across apps or websites. The app does not send your data to any server.

Alarms

With your permission, alarms are scheduled through Apple's AlarmKit and ring on your device. They are not sent through any server.

Purchases

Paid snoozes are bought through Apple's App Store; we never receive your payment details. The app only learns that a purchase succeeded and its price, so it can show the total you have spent. This total is stored on your device.

Children

Nine More is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Deleting an alarm in the app removes it. Deleting the app removes all data stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
